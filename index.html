How this works Every show's expected occupancy is min(1, (demand/10) × time-of-day factor) — morning ×0.60, afternoon ×0.85, evening/prime ×1.15, late night ×0.75. Expected revenue is capacity × occupancy × price × screen multiplier. The baseline rotates movies onto each screen back-to-back in a fixed order, ignoring demand. The optimizer ranks every feasible (movie, screen, start-time) candidate by revenue per occupied minute — the same rule that decides whether a long movie or a short one makes better use of a screen slot — and accepts candidates top-down, skipping any that overlap an already-placed show or exceed a movie's max-shows cap. This mirrors the project's C++ engine exactly; nothing here is guaranteed real-world revenue.
